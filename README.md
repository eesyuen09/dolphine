# 🐬 Dolphine

### Find a life, not just a room.

Dolphine is an AI relocation agent that helps users choose where to live by simulating future lifestyle outcomes instead of simply browsing property listings.

Rather than answering:

> "Which room should I rent?"

Dolphine answers:

> "What will my life look like if I live here?"

---

## 🚨 Problem

Property platforms help users find listings.

They do not help users understand the long-term consequences of where they choose to live.

A cheaper room may result in:

* Hundreds of hours of commuting every year
* Higher transportation costs
* Less time for exercise
* Worse daily convenience
* Lower overall quality of life

Housing is one of the biggest decisions people make, yet users are forced to manually evaluate these trade-offs.

---

## 💡 Solution

Dolphine acts as an AI relocation advisor.

Users provide:

* Workplace or school location
* Budget
* Transport preferences
* Lifestyle priorities

Dolphine then:

1. Understands user priorities
2. Evaluates candidate housing options
3. Simulates commute and lifestyle outcomes
4. Calculates trade-offs
5. Recommends the best-fit location
6. Explains the reasoning

---

## 🎯 Example

### User

```text
Workplace: Kent Ridge
Budget: S$1,500
No car
Gym 4x/week
Prefers quiet environment
Hybrid work arrangement
```

### Recommendation

🏆 Queenstown

**Why?**

* Saves ~230 commute hours/year compared to Jurong East
* Better gym accessibility
* Better MRT connectivity
* Strong food and convenience options

**Trade-off**

* Costs ~S$350/month more in rent

## ✨ Core Features

### Adaptive Lifestyle Scoring

Converts user preferences into personalized weights.

Example:

```text
Commute: 30%
Budget: 20%
Gym Access: 20%
Food & Convenience: 15%
Quietness: 15%
```

---

### Commute Intelligence

Calculates:

* Travel time
* Transport cost
* Annual commute burden

---

### Neighbourhood Intelligence

Evaluates:

* MRT accessibility
* Food options
* Gyms
* Supermarkets
* Parks
* Clinics

---

### Trade-Off Analysis

Shows what users gain and lose.

Example:

```text
Queenstown vs Jurong East

+230 hours/year saved
+Better gym access

-S$350/month higher rent
```

---

### Future Life Simulation

Generates a realistic preview of daily life.

```text
Monday

8:15 AM Leave Home
8:27 AM Arrive Kent Ridge

6:15 PM Gym Nearby
7:30 PM Dinner Nearby
```
